03 / Method

What the model does—and does not do.

The compact U-Net receives the pre-fire image, current image, their spectral difference, both clear-pixel masks, and elapsed days since ignition. Large scenes are evaluated in overlapping 256 × 256 tiles and combined in the browser.

This is burned-area segmentation, not fire-spread forecasting. It estimates the burned extent already visible in the current observation. Clouds, smoke, sensor differences, poor alignment, and imagery outside the training distribution can produce errors.
